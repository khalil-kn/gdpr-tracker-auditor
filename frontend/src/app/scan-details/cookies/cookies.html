What is checked

Security attributes are checked on cookies the site needs to work: sessions, carts and the consent record. Without Secure the browser would also send the cookie over plain http; without SameSite it relies on the browser default; without HttpOnly page scripts can read it.

Tracking cookies are reported by when they were set and how long they are kept, because their attributes are chosen by the vendor. Cookie values are never stored.
